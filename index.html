Questions Everyone Keeps Asking

How soon after a car accident should I see a chiropractor in Kent?

As soon as possible — ideally within 72 hours of the accident. The sooner you establish a medical record of your injury, the stronger your documentation is for both treatment and any potential insurance or legal claim. Waiting weeks or months gives insurance companies room to argue that your injury wasn't serious or wasn't caused by the accident.

Does PIP insurance cover chiropractic care in Washington State?

Yes. Washington State PIP (Personal Injury Protection) coverage typically covers chiropractic care related to a car accident injury. The specifics depend on your policy limits, but most standard Washington auto policies include at least $10,000 in PIP coverage for medical expenses. A clinic experienced with accident cases will handle the billing directly — you shouldn't have to manage that process yourself.

What's the difference between a regular chiropractor and a car accident chiropractor?

A general chiropractor is trained to treat spinal and musculoskeletal conditions broadly. A car accident chiropractor has specific experience managing whiplash, soft tissue injuries, and the documentation requirements of personal injury cases — including PIP billing, injury reports for legal proceedings, and coordination with attorneys. The clinical skill sets overlap, but the administrative and documentation experience is completely different.

Can a chiropractor help with whiplash after a car accident?

Yes, and chiropractic is actually one of the most evidence-supported treatments for whiplash-associated disorders. Cervical manipulation, soft tissue therapy, and guided rehabilitation can significantly reduce pain, restore range of motion, and prevent chronic dysfunction. The key is starting treatment promptly and working with a provider who understands the specific injury pattern.

Will seeing a chiropractor affect my car accident settlement?

Positively, in almost all cases. Having a documented treatment record from a qualified chiropractor establishes the nature and extent of your injuries, which supports a higher settlement value. Insurance companies routinely lowball accident victims who don't have medical documentation — proper chiropractic records are one of the most important tools your attorney will use in negotiations.

How many chiropractic visits do I typically need after a car accident?

It varies significantly depending on the severity of the injury. Mild whiplash might resolve in four to eight weeks of regular treatment. More significant spinal injuries can require three to six months of consistent care. A good chiropractor will give you a realistic initial estimate at your first appointment and adjust based on how you're responding to treatment.

What if the other driver's insurance is trying to get me to settle quickly?

Don't sign anything before you've completed treatment and have a full medical record documenting your injuries and recovery. Quick settlement offers are almost always low offers — insurers know that people in pain and financial stress are more likely to accept less than they deserve. Get your injuries documented first, talk to a personal injury attorney, and let the medical record do the work.

Is chiropractic care safe for car accident injuries?

Yes, when performed by a licensed and experienced chiropractor who takes a thorough injury history before beginning treatment. A qualified provider will screen for any contraindications — like fractures or certain vascular conditions — before performing any manipulation. This is exactly why the intake process matters so much: a rushed or superficial intake is a red flag.
